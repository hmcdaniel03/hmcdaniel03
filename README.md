## What I work on

Most of my projects live in private repos, but here's the gist:

- **Trading** — `futurestrader`, a futures trading bot, and `tradingstats` for tracking how my strategies actually perform.
- **Automation** — `medusa` and `agent_trading`, plus smaller experiments hooking LLMs into things I do often.
- **Work tools** — `bid_tracker` and `itbparser` for managing and parsing job bids, and `project_manager_suite`.

I'm self-taught and still learning, so most of this is me figuring things out as I build them.
