## 🧪 What I'm building

> 🔒 Most of these live in private repos for now — here's what's cooking in the lab.

### 🤖 AI Agents & Automation
- **`medusa`** — a multi-agent coding harness: one *boss* head coordinating many worker *snakes* that plan, code, and review in parallel.
- **`agent_trading`** — autonomous agents that research strategies and act on the markets.
- **`hermes-agent-sync`** — the coordination layer that keeps a fleet of agents in lockstep.
- **`gptexperimentation`** — my sandbox for poking at LLMs and seeing what breaks.

### 📈 Markets & Trading
- **`futurestrader`** — a Python futures-trading engine/bot.
- **`tradingstats`** — analytics & performance stats for trading strategies.

### 🧰 Real-world tooling
- **`bid_tracker`** · **`itbparser`** — turning messy bid documents into clean, structured, trackable data.
- **`project_manager_suite`** — a TypeScript project-management toolkit.
